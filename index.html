About關於 A8

A8 Recording is a production house and songwriting label built to balance mainstream appeal with experimental sound. Founded in New York, we now write, produce, and score across borders.

Full studio production — from the first demo to final master — paired with original songwriting and industry-standard engineering for international release, broadcast, and screen. Rooted in jazz, composition, and music technology training from The New School and NYU, A8 fuses chart-ready hooks with avant-garde, artist-defining records, and extends into interactive scoring for modern games.

A8 Recording NYC 致力於在主流吸引力與實驗聲響之間取得平衡的音樂製作廠牌與詞曲創作工作室。發跡於紐約，如今作品跨越國界與領域。

我們提供從Demo錄音到最終母帶工程的完整錄音室製作，結合原創詞曲與創作，為國際串流、廣播與影視市場量身打造。團隊承 The New School 與 NYU 的爵士、作曲與音樂科技專業背景，作品游走於商業金曲與前衛、定義藝人風格與創意之間，並持續拓展至遊戲互動配樂領域。

Now booking songwriting, composition, and production sessions — for artists, labels, film, and games.

現正接受 詞曲創作、作曲與製作案件 預約 — 藝人、唱片公司、影視與遊戲專案。
