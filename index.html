About關於 A8

A full production house and songwriting label, A8 Recording bridges commercial mainstream appeal with experimental sound. We deliver full-suite studio production—from initial tracking to final master—alongside original songwriting and lyric architecture optimized for international release, broadcast, and screen. Backed by backgrounds in jazz, composition, and music technology from The New School and NYU, A8 balances chart-ready hooks with avant-garde, artist-defining records, alongside interactive scoring for modern games.

A8 Recording 始於紐約的音樂製作廠牌與詞曲創作工作室，作品橫跨流行、電子與實驗音樂。我們將完整的錄音製作 — 工程、混音與母帶 — 結合原創詞曲創作，專為國際標準串流、廣播與影視市場打造。團隊根植於 The New School 與 NYU 的作曲、爵士與音樂科技訓練，作品在商業與實驗性、定義藝人風格的創作之間自由游走，並持續拓展至遊戲互動與銀幕敘事配樂領域。
